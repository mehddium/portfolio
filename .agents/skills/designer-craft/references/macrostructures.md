# Macrostructures — 12 Non-Default Layout Archetypes

To eliminate the "cookie-cutter AI landing page" syndrome, choose one of these macrostructures based on project genre:

1. **Open Editorial Index (Paco Coursey / Linear)**
   - Widescreen container with fine hairline dividers (`divide-y divide-[#232635]`).
   - Split 5/7 or 4/8 columns: Metadata & title on the left, rich narrative & technical specifics on the right.
   - Zero card enclosures.

2. **Split-Screen Sticky (Brittany Chiang)**
   - 40% left column fixed to viewport: Identity, mission, active scroll navigation, social anchors.
   - 60% right column scrolling: Experience, projects, writing.
   - Subtle spotlight hover effects on natural typographic items.

3. **Architectural Matrix (Swiss Modernism / Dieter Rams)**
   - Strict mathematical grid lines without card borders.
   - Dense, high-information typography with bold anchors and generous horizontal breathing room.
   - Asymmetric column spans (e.g. 4-col context, 8-col active matrix).

4. **The Engineer's Workbench**
   - High-density tool directory with interactive filtering.
   - Tables with monospace metadata and clean sans-serif labels.
   - Focus on tangible metrics, RFC specs, and benchmarks.

5. **Broadsheet / Specimen Sheet**
   - Newspaper-grade typographic hierarchy.
   - Large display headline paired with disciplined editorial columns.
   - Zero rounded corners.

6. **The Minimalist Colophon**
   - Understated, ultra-clean personal portfolio.
   - Focused exclusively on 3-4 deep case studies with expansive whitespace.
   - Generous margins and quiet confidence.
